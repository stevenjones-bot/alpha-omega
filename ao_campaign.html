How it runs

Uniforms lead. Everything else rides along.Every note opens on the branded uniform store and carries one line about gifts, welcome kits and promo items on the same link.
Clicks get a person, fast.Anyone who clicks gets the "Thanks for clicking" cut within 48 hours, once — then Steven calls.
Nobody hears the same thing twice.People who click drop out of the later sends; people who open but never click get their own note.
Five-day rule.Every send is judged on day five, never sooner; the next one is shaped by what the last one taught.
